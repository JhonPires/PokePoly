# Prompts dos cenários de arena

As artes de Kanto, Johto e Hoenn foram recriadas em 17/10/2026 com a ferramenta integrada ImageGen. A arte de Kalos serviu apenas como referência de ângulo e composição. Os arquivos finais substituem `assets/arena/kanto.png`, `assets/arena/johto.png` e `assets/arena/hoenn.png`.

## Kanto

> Wide 16:9 battle arena background for PokéPoly. New 2D pixel-art image inspired by the Kalos arena's straight-on eye-level camera, detail and broad perspective floor. Dramatic indigo stone cavern and gym, amber lanterns, violet crystals and old masonry. Unobstructed fighting floor throughout the lower 55%, clear horizontal rear edge around 43% from the top, subtle circular battle emblem. Symmetric framing, room for two moving Pokémon. No characters, Pokémon, UI, text, logos, green or top-down camera.

## Johto

> Wide 16:9 battle arena background for PokéPoly. New 2D pixel-art image inspired by the Kalos arena's side-view camera, detail and floor coverage. Ancient temple courtyard at twilight with indigo roofs, violet flowering trees, amber lanterns and carved stone guardians. Unobstructed fighting floor throughout the lower 55%, clear horizontal rear edge around 43% from the top, subtle circular battle emblem. Symmetric framing, room for two moving Pokémon. No characters, Pokémon, UI, text, logos, green or top-down camera.

## Hoenn

> Wide 16:9 battle arena background for PokéPoly. New 2D pixel-art image inspired by the Kalos arena's straight-on eye-level camera, detail and broad perspective floor. Tropical volcanic coast with indigo cliffs, blue waterfalls, coral-orange glows, distant sea and twilight sky. Broad dry stone platform throughout the lower 55%, clear horizontal rear edge around 43% from the top, subtle circular battle emblem. No water or obstacles on the floor. Symmetric framing, room for moving Pokémon. No characters, Pokémon, UI, text, logos, green or top-down camera.
