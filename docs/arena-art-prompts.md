Around the OUTER edges only: Indigo Plateau-inspired carved stone walls, a few red-coral and purple rock accents, small amber lanterns. Pixel art inspired game background, crisp readable shapes, polished 16-bit/32-bit pixel art, no perspective tilt, no horizon, no sky. Keep the middle 70% of the floor low contrast for sprite readability. Palette: midnight blue, indigo, warm sandstone, coral, gold. Absolutely no green in any part of the image. High quality seamless-feeling game environment, no overlays.
